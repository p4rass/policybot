Example Company — Leave and Remote Work Policy
This is a fictional sample for testing Policybot, not an actual employer policy.

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested at least 7 calendar days in advance and approved by the reporting manager. Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next year and must be used by March 31. Unused leave beyond this limit expires.

Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Notify your manager before the start of your shift where possible. A medical certificate is required for absences of more than 2 consecutive working days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

Remote work
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Employees must remain available during core hours, 10 AM to 4 PM local time. New employees become eligible for remote work after completing 90 days of service. Roles requiring physical presence are not eligible.

Exceptions
Emergency annual leave requests may be submitted with less than 7 days' notice, subject to manager approval. Additional remote work days require written approval from the department head. This policy does not specify parental leave, salary, reimbursement, or overtime rules.
